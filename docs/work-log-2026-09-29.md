## Built for 0.9.5

**Trade Contacts**
- "Show NPC names in town" checkbox on the Search tab and in the `/fa` panel (replaces `/fa contacts scout`, which still works).
- "Only not visited" checkbox: crafting NPCs you've passed but not talked to, nearest first (replaces `todo`).
- Hover tooltips on both checkboxes; the names-in-town one shows how many NPCs you've seen. Clicking a checkbox label now ticks it, suite-wide.
- Chat line when you toggle names in town.
- NPCs you talked to with no shop or trainer window are saved as "visited, no list yet".
- One search row per trainer with the ranks they teach ("trains 16 · Apprentice"). "seen, not visited yet" is now "seen, talk to save".
- With "Only not visited" ticked, a hidden match is named ("Karolek matches, but you've already visited").
- Contacts tab rebuilt: town headers that fold, Zone picker (All zones, Where I am, one zone), Trade picker (All crafting, one profession, Trade goods, Other, Everyone). Counts follow the other picker. Innkeepers and class/riding trainers only under Everyone. Solid menus that stay inside the window.
- More non-crafting titles ignored (weapon masters, riding trainers, bag vendors, and others).
- Confirmed in game: waypoints work with and without TomTom; search results are nearest first.

**Crafting plans (Alchemy, Leatherworking)**
- The planner now simulates crafts one at a time. Materials you make yourself (Light Leather, cured hides) count toward skill-ups, and each step shows "+ N Light Leather".
- On a tie, the plan picks the recipe that uses fewer materials. Verified in game: the Cloak instead of the Vest cut Light Leather from 411 to 274 and scraps from 1,215 to 804.
- Learn from: only names trainers who teach Apprentice (Brawn skipped).

**Fishing**
- Skill, pole and swap weapons are per character. An alt without Fishing shows "not learned yet". Your main's pole carried over after the login-timing fix. Verified on both characters.

**All modules**
- A profession you unlearned no longer shows your old skill; main and Progress tabs say not learned (found when Herbalism was dropped on the mage).
- Plural fixes: "1 craft", "1 cook", "1 catch".
